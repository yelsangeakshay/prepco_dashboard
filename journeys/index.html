Student

The paths a student walks from the marketing site to Day 0, on desktop and on a phone.

Join and buy a plan

Student8 steps

Starts A student lands on theprep.co.in

Ends A paid plan and an open dashboard

  1. 1
    Marketing siteNo mockup, this step happens outside the app
    Outside the app

    Book a free session

    Every Book your free session button opens a Calendly slot picker. 30 minutes, no card.

  2. 2
    StudentS1

    Create an account

    Google or college email. A session booked before signing up is matched by email.

    If this goes differently: Forgot password or personal Gmail

  3. 3
    StudentS2

    Pick track, target and Day 0

    Finals or summers, one of six functions, and the Day 0 date. The screen says how many weeks of runway are left.

    If this goes differently: Fewer than 8 weeks to Day 0

  4. 4
    StudentE2

    Upload the current CV

    It becomes V1 and gets a first score. One checkbox lets panelists read the student's history. An unreadable file can be replaced or typed in.

  5. 5
    StudentS3

    Read the session summary

    Three roles the profile fits, the CV lines a panel will pull on, how two practice answers landed, and a suggested runway.

  6. 6
    StudentS4

    Choose a plan

    Basic, Premium or Elite at Season 1 prices, with seats left shown.

    If this goes differently: The cohort is full

  7. 7
    StudentE4Proposed

    Pay and get a receipt

    UPI, card or net banking. A failed payment holds the seat for 15 minutes.

    If this goes differently: The payment fails

  8. 8
    StudentS5

    Open the dashboard

    Day 0 countdown, the runway, the next session and this week's tasks.

Prepare for Day 0

Student8 steps

Starts The plan is paid and the dashboard is open

Ends The Day 0 result is recorded

  1. 1
    StudentS5

    Check the runway

    Done, next and locked stages from Week -8 to Day 0, with the tasks for this week.

  2. 2
    StudentS6

    Build the CV

    Every bullet is scored on a decision made, a number moved and a trade-off owned. The score splits into impact, clarity and defensibility.

  3. 3
    StudentS7

    Lock a version

    V4 is locked after the profiling call. Later changes go through a re-lock request, so nothing moves the night before a shortlist.

  4. 4
    StudentS8

    Draft answers

    The answer bank is built from the locked CV. Weak answers are flagged with Situation, Decision and Result.

  5. 5
    StudentS9

    Book the next panel

    P1, deep-dive, P2, group session, P3. Premium students pick slots first.

    If this goes differently: A session is cancelled, moved or missed

  6. 6
    StudentS10

    Read panel feedback

    A readiness score, a note per question and a short list of fixes. Flagged answers go back to the answer bank. Steps 4 to 6 repeat for P2 and P3.

  7. 7
    StudentE10

    Get reminders

    Session reminders, feedback ready, and any change to the runway or to Day 0.

    If this goes differently: Day 0 moves

  8. 8
    StudentE7

    Report the outcome

    Placed on Day 0, Day 1 or Day 2, or not yet.

    If this goes differently: After Day 0
