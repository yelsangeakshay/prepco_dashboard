What we still need to decide

The mockups had to assume a few things the website never states. Each decision below shows what the screens assume and where it appears. Edge cases and what is not designed yet follow.

Open decisions

Anything tagged Proposed in a screen comes from this list. Answer these and the screens can be finalised.

  1. 1

    Does a missed panel use up the session?

    Assumed in the mockups: The first no-show is free to rebook. After that it counts as used. A dropped call or broken link on our side never counts.

    Shows onE6See the journey
  2. 2

    How late can a student reschedule for free?

    Assumed in the mockups: Free up to 24 hours before the session. Inside 24 hours it counts as missed.

    Shows onE6See the journey
  3. 3

    Which payment provider, and how are GST invoices issued?

    Assumed in the mockups: Not decided. The checkout shows UPI, card and net banking as a placeholder, and the invoice list is a proposal.

    Shows onS4E4S14See the journey
  4. 4

    What is the Season 2 price for returning students?

    Assumed in the mockups: The website says returning students get season-2 pricing but not what it is. The screen shows a placeholder.

    Shows onE11See the journey
  5. 5

    Below how many weeks to Day 0 do we stop selling Premium?

    Assumed in the mockups: Under 8 weeks the app recommends Basic plus one top-up and says why. The exact cut-off is open.

    Shows onE3See the journey
  6. 6

    Who gets a seat first from the waitlist?

    Assumed in the mockups: First come, first served, with 24 hours to take the seat before it passes on. Priority for free-session attendees is the alternative.

    Shows onE5See the journey
  7. 7

    What happens to unused sessions if a student is placed early through a PPO or PPI?

    Assumed in the mockups: Not decided: credited, transferable or lost. The Day 0 outcome screen has a decision-needed marker.

    Shows onE7See the journey
  8. 8

    Is the product for SCMHRD only at launch, or for several colleges?

    Assumed in the mockups: The mockups assume one college and one Day 0 date per cohort. Several colleges need a Day 0 date per college and per-college seat caps.

    Shows onS2E10See the journey
  9. 9

    How are panelists paid?

    Assumed in the mockups: Not decided: per panel or monthly, and whether feedback turnaround is part of it. The roster shows turnaround as a metric.

    Shows onP1A6See the journey
  10. 10

    Who runs the free sessions?

    Assumed in the mockups: The mockups show the Prep Co team. Senior panelists running them is the alternative.

    Shows onS3A4See the journey
